1. The Fundamentals of Optics

Hover over the highlighted terms to learn their definitions.

Spherical Mirrors

A mirror whose reflecting surface is a part of a hollow sphere of glass. The geometric center is called the Pole (P).

If the inner curve reflects, it's a Concave Mirror (converging). If the outer bulge reflects, it's a Convex Mirror (diverging).

Spherical Lenses

A transparent material bound by two surfaces. The central point is the Optical Centre (O).

A lens thicker in the middle is a Convex Lens. A lens thinner in the middle is a Concave Lens.

The Focal Point

The Principal Focus (F) is where parallel rays of light meet. The distance between the Pole/Optical Centre and F is the Focal Length (f).

2. Cartesian Sign Convention

The most important rule for solving numericals. All distances are measured from the Pole (P) or Optical Centre (O).

-ve (Against Incident Light) +ve (Along Incident Light)
+ve (Above Axis) -ve (Below Axis)

Incident Light ➔
  • Object Distance (u): Always Negative (-).
  • Focal Length (f): Convex = Positive (+), Concave = Negative (-).
  • Image Height (h'): Real = Negative (-), Virtual = Positive (+).
